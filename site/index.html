What moves the estimate

Each line is the change in the estimate from that input, against a typical value: no flag, no coauthors, an average committee and filing window, and the party average for the author.

These are what the model has learned goes with success. They are not causal effects: a bill with coauthors may do better because it was already a consensus bill.

Method and limits

Trained on earlier sessions and tested on , a session it had not seen, the model ranked a signed bill above an unsigned one about of the time (AUC , where 0.5 is chance). The range shows how much the estimate moves with the data it learned from and between typical sessions. It cannot see a bill's merits, the Governor's priorities, opposition, or deals made along the way.
