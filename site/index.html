California Legislative Scorecard

Every legislator, sessions. Bills authored, signed and vetoed, rebuilt from the Legislature's official bulk data. Ranks are within each chamber.

Ranks are within each chamber: the Assembly (80 members) and the Senate (40) are ranked separately, so with both chambers shown each rank appears twice, marked Asm or Sen. Tied members share a rank and the next rank is skipped. Success rate (signed / introduced) favors members who file fewer bills, so raw counts sit beside it. Members marked partial joined or left mid-session, so their record covers part of the term. Budget Act bills, which are introduced under the budget chairs' names, are left out of counts and ranks by default; use the Budget bills toggle to include them. The last column shows how many each member has. Year views count introductions by filing year and outcomes by the year of the Governor's action.
