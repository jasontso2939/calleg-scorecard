California Legislative Scorecard

Every legislator, 2025-26 session. Bills authored, signed and vetoed, rebuilt from the Legislature's official bulk data. Ranks are within each chamber.

Success rate (signed / introduced) favors members who file fewer bills, so raw counts sit beside it. Members marked partial joined or left mid-session, so their record covers part of the term. Budget Act bills, which are introduced under the budget chairs' names, are left out of counts and ranks by default; use the Budget bills toggle to include them. The last column shows how many each member has. Year views count introductions by filing year and outcomes by the year of the Governor's action.
Not affiliated with any office or legislator.
Source: California Legislative Information bulk export (leginfo). Pipeline, tests and methodology are in the repository. Not an endorsement of any legislator.
