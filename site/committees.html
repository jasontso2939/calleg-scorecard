Committee Rosters

Who sat on each policy and fiscal committee, who chaired it, and how members voted, year by year since 2007, from the Legislature's own roll calls.

Every committee roll call lists all of the committee's members on that date, with the chair first and the vice chair second, so rosters here are dated and year-specific. Members who appear on fewer than half of a year's hearings are marked part of year or substitute: they joined or left mid-year, or sat in for an absent member.
