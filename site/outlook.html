Bill Outlook

Estimate the chance a proposed bill is signed, from what is known when it is introduced. A statistical model trained on every legislator's 2025-26 bills.

Read this as a rough guide. On bills it had not seen, the model ranks a signed bill above an unsigned one about of the time (AUC , where 0.5 is a coin flip). It cannot see a bill's merits, the Governor's priorities, opposition, or deals made along the way. It is one session of data.

How well the model is calibrated

Not in the model yet

Committee membership and votes (whether the author or coauthors sit on the committee, the chair's record on similar bills, how members voted), bill text beyond the subject line, opposition and support letters, and earlier sessions. These are the next additions; see the roadmap in the repository.
