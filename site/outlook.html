Bill Outlook

Estimate the chance a proposed bill clears each step to the Governor's signature, from what is known when it is introduced. A statistical model trained on every legislator's bills in .

Read this as a rough guide. Trained on earlier sessions and tested on , a session it had not seen, the model ranked a signed bill above an unsigned one about of the time (AUC , where 0.5 is a coin flip). The range shows how much the estimate moves with the data it learned from and between typical sessions. It cannot see a bill's merits, the Governor's priorities, opposition, or deals made along the way.
